# Blog

## REDFOR Vehicle Update - 25/07/2026

RHS is extremely excited to announce some of the development work StoKer has been up to recently! What started as a project to integrate new 3D assets has evolved far beyond the initial scope; StoKer is now aiming to add a range of new assets and frameworks that can be used across a number of vehicles in a modular fashion.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Kurganbets-25 is in development, bringing protected mobility and firepower as the most modern, if somewhat still in development Infantry Fighting Vehicle.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (192).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

StOker is also hard at work on Russia's most modern variant of their in-service MBT, the T-90M.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (185).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

StoKer has also worked on a variety of modular frameworks which can be applied to other armored vehicles, including:

* Active Protection Systems which will allow both soft kill and hard kill mechanisms,
* Engine Smoke System (ESS), allowing vehicles equipped with the system to rapidly produce a dynamic amount of smoke to conceal a force,
* Fire Suppression: Manual or automatically extinguish fires to prevent ammunition detonations and extend the survivability of a vehicle,
* Guided Missiles: Adding a framework for much more realistic missiles including TV Guidance, Electronic/Optronic-InfraRed, and Laser tracking.,
* A hunter killer system, allowing a vehicle commander to independently acquire targets from the gunner and hand off the contact for engagement,
* RADAR, adding additional situational awareness beyond optical systems,\
  Thermal imaging allowing modern thermal optics which will enhance realism and target acquisition,
* Last but not least, a ballistic computer or fire control system, enabling lead indication which could be incorporated into future air defence platforms
