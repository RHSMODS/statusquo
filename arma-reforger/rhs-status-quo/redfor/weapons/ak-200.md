# AK-200

{% hint style="info" %}
First listed in the **0.12** changelog.
{% endhint %}

5.45×39mm assault rifle. The AK-200 is a modernized assault rifle based on the AK-74M and AK-100 series, featuring updated ergonomics, integrated Picatinny rails, and a folding telescopic stock.
