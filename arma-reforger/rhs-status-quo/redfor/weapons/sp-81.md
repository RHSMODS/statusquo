# SP-81

{% hint style="info" %}
Introduced in **0.2.414**
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/sp81" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

26×75mm signal pistol. The SP-81 is the result of a Soviet effort to replace older SPsH-44 signal pistols—it is smaller, lighter, and cheaper to produce than its predecessor.
