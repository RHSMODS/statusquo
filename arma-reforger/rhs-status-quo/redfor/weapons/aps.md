# APS

{% hint style="info" %}
Introduced in 0.2.79
{% endhint %}

9×18mm select-fire pistol. The APS, or 'Stechkin', is a Soviet pistol introduced in 1951 for use with artillery, mortar, tank, and aircraft crews where a full-sized assault rifle was deemed impractical.
