# AKM

<!-- TODO: confirm the version that introduced the AKM and add the "Introduced in" hint -->

7.62×39mm assault rifle. The AKM is a modernized variant of the AK-47, introduced in 1959. It has a stamped sheet-metal receiver and a slant muzzle brake.

<!-- TODO: screenshot of the AKM, three-quarter view (akm-side.webp) -->

## Description

The AKM has a wood stock, a wood handguard and a 6P20 0-20 muzzle brake. The AKMN variant has a side rail for night sights.

The rear sight leaf has settings from 100 m to 1000 m and a battle setting (П). Each setting raises the leaf to a different angle. The top of the front post aligns with the top of the rear sight leaf at all settings.

## Usage

Use the sight settings to set the range to the target:

1. Aim down the sights.
2. Change the sight setting to the range to the target. The rear sight leaf moves.
3. Put the top of the front post level with the top of the rear sight leaf.
4. Put the target on the top of the front post.
5. Fire.

{% hint style="info" %}
Use the battle setting (П) for targets up to 300 m. The battle setting is the same as the 300 m setting. At shorter ranges, the bullet hits a little above the point of aim.
{% endhint %}
