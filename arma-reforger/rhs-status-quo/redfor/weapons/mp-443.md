# MP-443

{% hint style="info" %}
First listed in the **0.7** changelog.
{% endhint %}

9×19mm semi-automatic pistol. The MP-443, also known as 'PYa' for 'Pistolet Yarygina', is one of the standard-issue handguns of the Russian Armed Forces.
