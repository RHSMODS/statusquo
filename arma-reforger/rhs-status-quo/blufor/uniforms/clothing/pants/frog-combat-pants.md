# FROG Combat Pants

The FROG (Flame-Resistant Organizational Gear) combat pants are part of the US Marine Corps' uniform system, designed to protect personnel against flash fires and heat hazards in combat environments.

## Specifications

| Category | Data |
| --- | --- |
| Name in game | FROG Combat Pants - MARPAT-WD |
| Faction | US forces (BLUFOR) |

## Variants

The item is available in these versions:

* FROG Combat Pants - MARPAT-WD
* FROG Combat Pants - AOR2
* FROG Combat Pants - MARPAT-D
* FROG Combat Pants - MCAM
