The UH-1Y 'Venom' is a twin-engine utility helicopter operated by the USMC, developed under the H-1 Upgrade Program to replace the UH-1N. It shares 84% component commonality with the AH-1Z Viper and serves in troop transport, assault support, and casualty evacuation roles.
