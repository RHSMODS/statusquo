# M1151

{% hint style="info" %}
Introduced in **0.2.414**
{% endhint %}

The M1151A1 is an up-armored variant of the HMMWV, fitted with a more powerful engine, upgraded suspension and brakes, and increased armor protection. Configured as a weapons platform with a roof-mounted gunner station.
