# KAC rail covers

Polymer rail cover that protects Picatinny rails while providing a non-slip gripping surface. Standard issue alongside the M4 and M16A4 in US service.

## Specifications

| Category | Data |
| --- | --- |
| Name in game | Rib Rail Cover (M4A1 Block II) - Black & Taupe |
| Weight | 100 g |

## Variants

The item is available in these versions:

* Rib Rail Cover (M4A1 Block II) - Black & Taupe
* 6 Rib Rail Cover
* 6 Rib Rail Cover - Taupe
* Rib Rail Cover (M27)
* Rib Rail Cover (M27) - Taupe
* Rib Rail Cover (Kit 2) - Black & Taupe
* Rib Rail Cover (M16A4)
* Rib Rail Cover (M16A4) - Taupe
* Rib Rail Cover (M4)
* Rib Rail Cover (M4) - Taupe
